# Project Chronos — Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

Project Chronos is an independent, open scientific research initiative built around collaboration, curiosity, scientific integrity, and the advancement of human knowledge.

We are committed to creating an environment where everyone can participate respectfully, regardless of nationality, experience, educational background, professional status, or technical expertise.

We welcome researchers, engineers, developers, students, and independent contributors.

## 2. Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Welcome constructive criticism and alternative perspectives.
- Evaluate ideas based on evidence and reasoning.
- Give appropriate credit to original contributions.
- Respect intellectual property and licensing requirements.
- Communicate clearly and honestly.
- Help maintain an inclusive and productive research environment.

## 3. Scientific Discussion

Scientific disagreement is encouraged.

Participants should be free to question hypotheses, challenge conclusions, and identify errors without fear of personal attacks.

However:

- Criticism should address ideas, methods, and evidence rather than individuals.
- Unsupported claims should be identified as such.
- Sources should be provided when making important scientific claims.
- Speculative ideas must not be presented as established facts.
- Research contributions should remain open to independent examination.

No individual or contributor has automatic authority over scientific truth.

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation.
- Discrimination or personal attacks.
- Deliberate misinformation or falsification of research results.
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Unauthorized disclosure of private information.
- Disruptive behavior that prevents constructive collaboration.
- Deliberate misuse of project infrastructure or computing resources.

## 5. Attribution and Intellectual Contributions

Contributors should receive appropriate recognition for their work.

Research results, software, documentation, and other contributions should retain appropriate attribution and comply with their applicable licenses.

No contributor should claim ownership of another person's work.

## 6. Responsible Use of Project Infrastructure

Participants must respect the security and operational integrity of Chronos infrastructure.

Unauthorized access, malicious code, resource abuse, and attempts to compromise other nodes are prohibited.

Autonomous AI systems must operate within their authorized permissions.

## 7. Reporting Problems

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it through the project's designated communication channels or contact the project maintainers.

Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as reasonably possible.

Retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith is not acceptable.

## 8. Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing violations.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, actions may include:

1. A private warning.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation.
3. Removal of inappropriate content.
4. Temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions should be fair, proportionate, and consistent.

## 9. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, discussions, communication channels, events, and other spaces officially associated with Project Chronos.

## 10. A Shared Responsibility

Project Chronos is intended to be a collaborative effort extending across institutions, countries, and generations.

Its scientific ambitions require not only technical excellence but also mutual respect, intellectual honesty, and responsible collaboration.

**We challenge ideas rigorously, treat people respectfully, and put the pursuit of knowledge above personal recognition.**
