# The Founding Manifesto of Project Chronos

**An Open Scientific Initiative for the Advancement of Knowledge and Humanity**

**Founded: November 5, 2026**

## 1. The Vision

Human knowledge is one of humanity's greatest achievements. Yet scientific progress remains distributed across individuals, institutions, generations, and limited periods of time.

Project Chronos is founded on a simple ambition: to develop an enduring scientific research infrastructure that can preserve knowledge, support autonomous discovery, and contribute to humanity's understanding of the universe.

We envision a future in which intelligent systems can continuously study, organize, evaluate, and expand scientific knowledge while collaborating with researchers across the world.

Our ambition extends beyond building another artificial intelligence system. We seek to investigate the deeper relationship between intelligence, information, time, and physical reality.

## 2. The Mission

Our mission is to advance scientific knowledge and artificial intelligence for the benefit of humanity.

We aim to:

- Develop autonomous AI systems capable of supporting structured scientific research.
- Establish persistent and reliable scientific memory.
- Build distributed networks of independently operated research nodes.
- Encourage collaboration between engineers, scientists, mathematicians, and independent researchers.
- Investigate the fundamental nature of time, information, and causality.
- Preserve scientific knowledge and make it accessible to future generations.

## 3. Scientific Integrity

Scientific integrity is the foundation of Project Chronos.

We recognize the difference between an interesting idea, a mathematical possibility, a theoretical prediction, and an experimentally verified discovery.

Our research will follow these principles:

1. Evidence must take priority over expectations.
2. Every important result should be reproducible whenever possible.
3. Hypotheses must remain open to criticism and rejection.
4. Theoretical possibilities must never be presented as established physical phenomena.
5. Failed experiments and negative results should be documented.
6. AI-generated conclusions must undergo independent scientific examination.
7. Research methods, assumptions, and limitations should be transparent.

Project Chronos must always be capable of questioning its own conclusions.

## 4. The Long-Term Research Ambition

One of the most ambitious questions motivating this initiative concerns the relationship between time and information.

Could information generated at a future point in time ever be physically received at an earlier point?

Modern physics provides profound theoretical frameworks for investigating spacetime, relativity, quantum information, and causality. Some mathematical solutions raise questions about unusual causal structures, but their physical realizability remains unresolved.

Project Chronos intends to investigate these questions rigorously.

We do not assume that backward information transfer is possible. We seek to understand whether it is consistent with physical law, whether any proposed mechanism could be tested, and whether the hypothesis must ultimately be rejected.

The pursuit of a difficult question is valuable even when its answer is negative.

## 5. Development Philosophy

Chronos will begin with accessible hardware and a modular software architecture.

The first instance will run on a Raspberry Pi 5. This is a starting platform, not a limitation on the project's future scale.

The system should be designed to migrate between hardware platforms, preserve its accumulated knowledge, and operate through independently maintained nodes.

Long-term continuity must not depend on one computer, one server, one individual, or one institution.

## 6. Collaboration and Openness

Project Chronos welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

We believe that meaningful scientific progress benefits from collaboration between:

- Universities and research institutions.
- Artificial intelligence laboratories.
- Physicists and mathematicians.
- Engineers and computer scientists.
- Independent developers and researchers.
- Individuals who are willing to contribute knowledge and effort.

The project should remain transparent, accessible, and open to constructive criticism.

Scientific merit, not institutional status, should determine the value of an idea.

## 7. Independence and Responsibility

Project Chronos is established as an independent research initiative.

Its scientific direction and conclusions must remain independent of financial sponsors, institutional interests, and personal ambitions.

Resources and partnerships may help the project grow, but they must never determine its scientific conclusions.

Artificial intelligence must be developed responsibly, with appropriate human oversight, security, and consideration of its consequences.

## 8. A Commitment Across Generations

Project Chronos is intended to be a long-term effort.

Its development may extend beyond the lifetime of its founders and contributors. Therefore, documentation, reproducibility, preservation, and knowledge transfer are essential from the beginning.

Future contributors should be able to understand not only what the project achieved, but also why decisions were made, which assumptions were tested, and which approaches failed.

We build not only for the present, but for those who will continue the work in the future.

## 9. The Founding Principle

Project Chronos does not belong to a single person or generation in its ultimate purpose.

Its purpose is to contribute to the collective advancement of humanity.

We begin with limited resources, an ambitious question, and the conviction that knowledge should be preserved, shared, questioned, and expanded.

**The future should inherit more knowledge than the present possesses.**

**The work begins with one node. The vision belongs to humanity.**
